Track record · the desk grades itself

Yesterday’s call, scored — and what it learned

Every locked call is graded the next session against what gold actually did. Those results feed back to correct the confidence — the one kind of “learning” an efficient market allows. It makes the numbers honest; it doesn’t make the direction sharper.

The model can’t see the future

Integrity · failure behaviour

When a source fails, the desk goes dark — not stale

A visibly broken run is safe; a silently degraded one that still looks confident is the dangerous failure mode. On any pipeline failure the desk shows the left card and stops — it never shows the right one.
